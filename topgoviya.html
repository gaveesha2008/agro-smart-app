About Lanka Price Monitor

Lanka Price Monitor (TopGoviya.lk) provides daily vegetable, fish, and rice prices in Sri Lanka. Track wholesale & retail prices at Dambulla, Pettah & Narahenpita markets.

Features

Farmer profit calculator · Trader margin analysis · 4-year price history · YoY seasonal trends · AI market insights · Gap & opportunity charts

Commodities

Vegetables · Rice · Fish · Fruits · Onions · Potatoes · Green Chilli · Lime · Tomato · Beans · Carrot · Coconut · 43+ items daily

Data Source

Data: Central Bank of Sri Lanka Daily Price Report | Free | No login | Updated daily at 5:00 PM Sri Lanka time
